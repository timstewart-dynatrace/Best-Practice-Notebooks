M2S - Managed to SaaS Migration

Guide for migrating from Dynatrace Managed to Dynatrace SaaS.

Recommended: For the best experience, import the notebooks into your Dynatrace tenant. Each one is available as a Dynatrace notebook (JSON) file, and its DQL queries run against your own environment's data.

Notebook Lineup

  1. Step 1 — Discover: Understand SaaS Differences — Understanding why you're migrating, the benefits of SaaS, and inventorying your Managed environment
  2. Step 2 — Strategize: Define Your Migration Approach — Planning your migration strategy, phasing, and execution approach
  3. Step 3 — Design: Create Target Architecture — Designing your target SaaS architecture and configuration
  4. Step 4 — Prepare: Readiness and Pre-Migration — Assessing readiness and preparing for migration
  5. Step 5 — Execute: Migrate Configuration and Agents — Migrating configurations and redirecting agents to SaaS
  6. Step 6 — Integrate: Reconnect Integrations — Reconnecting cloud integrations and third-party tools
  7. Step 7 — Enable: User Enablement and Communication — User training, support, and operations handover
  8. Step 8 — Expand: Adopt New SaaS Capabilities — Discovering and adopting new SaaS-only capabilities
  9. Step 9 — Optimize: Validate, Optimize, and Decommission — Validating the migration, optimizing the environment, and decommissioning Managed
  10. [LAB] Terraform for Managed-to-SaaS Migration — Appendix lab: migrating with the Terraform provider — bulk vs iterative export, Managed-source auth and scopes, entity-ID preservation via oneagentctl, wave-ordered apply
  11. Best Practice Summary — Definitive reference of all best practices from the M2S series

Usage

  1. Open a notebook from the lineup below. Each one is also available as a Dynatrace notebook (JSON) to import into your tenant, so its queries run against your own data, and as a PDF for print.
  2. Follow the numbered sequence for a complete migration journey.
