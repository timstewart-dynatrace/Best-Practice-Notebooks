What Actually Carries Over When We Migrate to a New Tenant? — Migration teams ask this in a dozen shapes — do entity IDs stay the same, can we reuse the baselines, can we send to both tenants at once — and all of them are answered by sorting the thing in question into one of three classes: configuration (authored, so it moves and gets rewritten), identity (platform-minted, so it is re-minted on arrival) and accumulated state (learned or secret, so there is no tool at all).
