Why This Order

Step Reading Time Why this position in the sequence
1 ALERT 1 week Alerting strategy underpins all downstream operations. Establish detection and routing patterns first.
2 DASH 1–2 weeks Everything else needs dashboards to be visible. You alert against what you see; you automate around what you can dashboard; you trust Davis when its findings are visible somewhere.
3 WFLOW 2–3 weeks Alert response. Needs dashboards to point at and incidents to route.
4 SLO 1 week Reliability targets. Inform burn-rate alerting in WFLOW.
5 AUTOM 3–6 weeks Configuration automation, GitOps. Needs dashboards and workflows as deliverables — automating their lifecycle.
6 AIOPS 2–4 weeks Davis intelligence layered on top. Needs all of the above to be enriched and acted on.
7 FINOPS 1–2 weeks Cost optimization ongoing; informs roadmap priorities.

You can read partially out of order, but the reasoning above is why each builds on the previous.
